# AI Platform Lab

A personal engineering lab for learning platform operations and AI-assisted engineering through observable experiments.

## Language

**Platform Lab**:
The disposable environment in which the engineer develops and demonstrates platform engineering capabilities.
_Avoid_: Production platform

**Software Factory**:
The engineering workflow through which the human engineer and AI coding agents turn objectives into reviewed, verified changes.
_Avoid_: Autonomous agent company

**Delivery System**:
The mechanisms that apply approved changes to the Platform Lab and report their results.

**Human Demonstration**:
Evidence that the engineer can explain, diagnose, or recover a capability using documentation without an agent supplying the answers.
_Avoid_: Agent self-assessment

**Recovery Kit**:
The inventoried material outside the Platform Lab that, together with the repository, is sufficient to recreate the lab after its complete loss.
